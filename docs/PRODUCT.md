# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and early-career builders looking for relevant next steps such as hackathons, fellowships, internships, scholarships, and conferences. They are busy, ambitious, and do not want to repeatedly search scattered sources.

## Product Purpose

ScoutDeck watches across the web for opportunities that match a person's goals and sends a focused weekly brief of five worthwhile options. The waitlist page's job is to make a visitor understand the value quickly and leave an email address for early access.

## Positioning

Unlike a generic opportunity feed or search box, ScoutDeck evaluates opportunities against a person's evolving "signal"—their skills, interests, projects, and aspirations—and explains why each item is relevant.

## Operating Context

Visitors may be planning projects, applying for programs, building a portfolio, or looking for a first meaningful career move. Relevant opportunities are distributed across many sources and expire quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current surface is a static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript site. It uses two Formspree-backed waitlist forms, email validation, optional interest selection, loading/error/success states, a theme toggle, and an accessible mobile menu. No backend or build step is present.

## Brand Commitments

Use the ScoutDeck name and preserve the promise of a personal scout that finds five focused opportunities with context. The user has requested a complete visual replacement that avoids generic AI-generated landing-page conventions and makes deliberate use of motion.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing waitlist copy and interactions in `index.html`, `styles.css`, and `script.js`; the Formspree endpoint recorded in `README.md`. No verified testimonials, customer counts, or commercial outcome claims are available and must not be invented.

## Product Principles

- Make useful opportunity discovery feel personal rather than algorithmically opaque.
- Prioritize a small, explainable set of options over an endless feed.
- Reduce the overhead of keeping watch across fragmented sources.
- Make the waitlist action clear, low-friction, and trustworthy.
